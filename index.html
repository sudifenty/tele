🛡 Tele

Parent sign in

Tele shows the call log and texts from your child's phone only to you. Your child's phone shows a permanent supervision notice at all times.

Create your family

This creates a private family space. Next, you link your child's phone with a 6-digit code.

Live location

Recent points

Location is shared while supervision is on. The child's phone shows a separate notice while it is shared. You can pause sharing here; the child's phone then shows that sharing is paused.
